A demo of detaching POS client↔server communication via a Mediator. Instead of the POS Client talking to the Backend for everything, a Mediator (S3-compatible object storage on UpCloud) carries the bulk of the traffic — config/catalog downloads and receipt uploads — while the Backend is only touched briefly to log in and to hand out short-lived upload URLs.
